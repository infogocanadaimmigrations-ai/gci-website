Costs

UK tuition fees for Indian students (2026)

Real ranges, so you can budget before you apply — not after.

UK tuition fees vary more by university and subject than by anything else. The table below shows the fees Indian students most commonly pay in 2026. Figures in ₹ are converted at roughly ₹110 to the pound and will move with the exchange rate — treat them as indicative, not quotes.

CourseTypical fees / yearApprox. in ₹ / year
Bachelor's degree (3 years)£10,000 – £28,000₹11 – 31 lakh
1-year Master's£11,000 – £25,000₹12 – 27.5 lakh
MBA£30,000 – £80,000+₹33 – 88 lakh
PhD / research£15,000 – £25,000₹16.5 – 27.5 lakh

Living costs, and the money you must show

Budget a further £9,200 – £16,000 a year (roughly ₹10 – 17.5 lakh) for rent, food and travel. London is materially more expensive than the rest of the country.

Separately, UK Visas and Immigration requires you to evidence living costs when you apply: £1,529 per month if you study in London, or £1,171 per month elsewhere, capped at nine months (about £10,539). This is on top of your tuition, and the funds must sit in an eligible account for 28 consecutive days.

So a realistic first-year outlay for a one-year Master's outside London is commonly in the ₹25 – 40 lakh region once tuition, living costs, flights and the visa fee are counted. A cheaper course does not always mean a cheaper year — a London postcode can cost more than the tuition saving.

English tests

IELTS requirements — and options if your band is low

Most UK universities ask for IELTS 6.0 to 6.5 overall for a degree-level course, and many also set a minimum for each individual band. Degree courses sit at CEFR level B2; courses below degree level are usually B1.

If your score is below the requirement

A lower band does not automatically end the plan, but it does narrow your options. Depending on the university and the course, these routes may be open:

  • ✓Foundation or pathway programmes — several accept 5.5 overall, then progress you into the degree.
  • ✓PTE Academic or TOEFL — widely accepted alternatives; some students score better on a computer-based format.
  • ✓Medium of Instruction (MOI) — a few universities accept proof that your previous study was in English, subject to their own policy.
  • ✓University-run English assessments — internal tests offered by some institutions.
  • ✓Retaking the test — often the simplest route, and worth doing properly rather than rushing.

Two things to be clear about. Admission requirements and visa requirements are assessed separately — clearing a university's English condition does not by itself satisfy UKVI. And whether any alternative applies depends entirely on the individual university's published policy, which changes. We will tell you honestly which of your target courses accept your current score before you spend money on applications.

Postgraduate

The 1-year Master's in the UK

The single biggest reason Indian students choose the UK is that most taught masters degrees finish in 12 months rather than two years. One year of tuition and one year of living costs instead of two changes the total cost of the qualification substantially, even when the annual fee looks higher than elsewhere.

Who a one-year Master's suits

It works best if you already hold a relevant Bachelor's degree, want to specialise rather than change field entirely, and are comfortable with an intensive year — UK Master's programmes are compressed, with dissertations running through the summer.

Typical entry requirements are a Bachelor's degree with roughly 55–60% or above (varies by university and by subject), IELTS 6.0–6.5, and a statement of purpose that connects your existing qualification to the course you have chosen. Some universities also want relevant work experience for MBA and management Master's programmes.

Students searching for a masters in the UK under a tight budget should look outside London and at the newer universities, where a full masters programme can sit near the lower end of the range above. Popular masters fields among students we place include business and management, computer science and data analytics, engineering, healthcare and nursing, accounting and finance, and law. If you are weighing a Master's against a shorter course, the Master's route is usually the stronger visa file as well — the course fit is easier to justify.

Timing

Intakes: September 2026 and January 2027

UK universities run two main intakes, plus a small May intake at some institutions.

September / autumn intake is the larger one, with the widest choice of courses, scholarships and university accommodation. Applications generally open the previous autumn; strong candidates apply between November and March.

January / winter intake is smaller but genuinely useful if you missed September, need extra time for an English retake, or want longer to arrange funds. Applications typically run from roughly July to November.

Working backwards from a September 2026 start

  • ✓10–12 months before — shortlist courses, sit IELTS or PTE, begin building a documented funds history.
  • ✓6–8 months before — submit applications; offers usually follow in four to eight weeks.
  • ✓3–4 months before — accept your offer, pay the deposit, receive your CAS.
  • ✓2–3 months before — funds seasoned 28 days, then submit the visa application.
  • ✓4–6 weeks before — accommodation, flights, forex and pre-departure.

The most common reason a student misses an intake is not a refusal — it is starting the funds trail and the English test too late.

Funding

Scholarships available to Indian students

UK scholarships are competitive and rarely cover everything, but they are real and worth applying for early. Deadlines usually fall well before course deadlines.

Government and major awards

Chevening Scholarships — UK government funded, for one-year Master's degrees. Covers tuition, a monthly living stipend (in the region of £1,690 a month for London), flights and visa costs; total value commonly £30,000–£50,000 or more. Requires substantial work experience and a strong leadership record. Applications typically open in August and close in early November.

Commonwealth Scholarships — for Master's and PhD study, aimed at candidates whose work will contribute to development in their home country. Covers tuition, airfare and a living allowance.

University awards — where most students actually succeed

Individual universities offer merit-based fee reductions, commonly in the range of £1,000 to £7,000 off tuition, sometimes more for outstanding academic records. These are far less competitive than Chevening and are where most of our students realistically save money. Many are awarded automatically on the strength of your application; others need a separate form and a short essay.

There are also subject-specific and regional awards, and early-payment or alumni discounts at some institutions. We will tell you which awards your profile is genuinely competitive for rather than encouraging you to apply for everything.

After you graduate

UK post-study work: the Graduate Route

The Graduate Route lets you stay in the UK to work, or look for work, after you complete your degree. You do not need a job offer to apply, and you are not tied to a single employer.

One change matters for anyone planning now: the Graduate Route is being reduced from two years to 18 months from January 2027. Whether you receive 24 months or 18 depends on when you complete your course and apply, so check the cut-off against your own graduation date rather than assuming.

Eighteen months is still workable — it is enough time to find graduate-level employment and, in the right field, to move onto a Skilled Worker visa. What it removes is slack. Start applying for jobs during your final term rather than after results, and choose a course with genuine employer demand behind it.

A word of realism: post-study work is permission to look for work, not a guarantee of a job or of settlement. The UK, Canada and Australia have all tightened these routes in recent years. Choose a course that is worth doing on its academic and career merit alone, so a further rule change does not undo your plan. You can read our fuller analysis in the UK Graduate Route guide.

FAQ

UK study questions we are asked most

How much does it cost to study in the UK from India?

For a one-year Master's outside London, most families should plan for roughly ₹25–40 lakh for the first year once tuition (£11,000–£25,000), living costs (£9,200–£16,000), flights and the visa fee are included. Bachelor's degrees run three years, so the total is higher even though the annual fee can be similar. MBAs are the most expensive at £30,000–£80,000 or more in tuition alone.

Which UK universities accept a 5.5 IELTS band?

Most degree courses ask for 6.0–6.5 overall, so 5.5 usually points towards a foundation or pathway programme, which several universities accept at 5.5 and which then progresses into the degree. A handful of institutions also consider PTE, TOEFL or a Medium of Instruction letter instead. Which specific universities accept what changes every intake and is set by each institution — send us your scoresheet and we will check your target courses against their current published policy.

Can I go to the UK after 12th?

Yes. You can apply directly to a Bachelor's degree after Class 12, typically needing around 60% or above and IELTS 6.0–6.5, though requirements vary by university and subject. If your marks or English are short of the requirement, an International Year One or foundation programme is the usual route and adds one year before the degree begins.

How long does a UK student visa take?

Standard processing for applications made outside the UK is around 15 working days — about three weeks. During peak season before the September intake it can stretch to three to six weeks, so file early rather than relying on the shortest quoted time. A priority service is available at extra cost at some visa application centres.

What is a CAS letter?

A CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) is the reference the university issues once you have accepted your offer and met its conditions, including any deposit. You cannot apply for a student visa without one. Two practical points: a CAS can only be used for one application, and it expires six months after it is issued — so timing it correctly against your visa filing matters.

Can you promise my visa will be approved?

No — and no consultant honestly can. Go Canada Immigrations is an independent private consultancy — we are not affiliated with UK Visas and Immigration or any government body, and we do not issue visas or decide applications. What we do is prepare a complete, honest, well-documented file and tell you plainly where it is weak before you submit. The decision rests entirely with UKVI.
